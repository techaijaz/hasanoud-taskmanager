Dono mein same

Neeche 4 tabs: Home, Tasks, Attendance (apna mahine ka record aur sudhaar ki request), Profile (details, documents, change request). Manager aur HR ko inke alawa extra tabs milenge. Woh agli screens mein dikhaunga.

Note: yeh sirf layout ka sketch hai. Rang, font aur design baad mein final honge. Naam aur tasks example ke liye hain.
